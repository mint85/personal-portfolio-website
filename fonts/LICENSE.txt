Self-hosted web fonts
=====================

These woff2 files are the latin subset of two open-source typefaces, served
locally instead of from Google Fonts. Both are licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1), which permits self-hosting and
redistribution.

Inter
  Files:   inter-400.woff2, inter-600.woff2, inter-800.woff2
  Author:  Rasmus Andersson
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Source:  https://github.com/rsms/inter

JetBrains Mono
  Files:   jetbrains-mono-400.woff2, jetbrains-mono-600.woff2
  Author:  JetBrains
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1
  Source:  https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

The woff2 subsets were obtained via Fontsource (https://fontsource.org),
which repackages the upstream OFL fonts. Full license text:
https://openfontlicense.org
